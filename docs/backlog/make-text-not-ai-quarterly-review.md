# make-text-not-ai: quarterly review of the marker rules

AI-writing tells rotate with model generations, roughly once a year for vocabulary and faster for
punctuation (delve peaked in 2023–24, em dashes dropped in GPT-5.x but not in Claude). The skill's
lists date from 2026-10-30. Next review is due 2027-01-30, then every three months.

What a review covers:
- Diff WP:AISIGNS (by-era vocabulary, "Historical indicators", new sections) and ВП:ПРГЕН
  against `skills/make-text-not-ai/references/markers.md`. Move faded markers to the density tier
  or drop them, and add new ones.
- New corpus or detector numbers (Kobak follow-ups, Pangram, writehuman) that move a §2 threshold,
  especially the em-dash rate per model.
- New "humanized" over-correction tells, which belong in principle 3 and the fragment row.
- Re-run the skill on one EN and one RU AI-written sample and fix any rule that turned out ambiguous.
- Update the date at the top of `references/sources.md`.

Lifecycle: the commit that lands a review deletes this file and adds a fresh one with the next due
date in its body.
